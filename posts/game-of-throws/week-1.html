Loveland Delivers Nothing to Osama Bin Loveland

The namesake tight end scored zero, Dippin Darts won with 29.8 points on the bench, and a kicker outscored four receivers.

The realm has spoken, and it said "why is that guy in your lineup?" Twelve houses left 145.5 points on their benches in Week 1. Two of them would have flipped their losses with a better lineup. One of them runs this blog. We'll get to that.

But first, the draft. It's over, the ink is dry, and some of you already owe the realm an explanation.

Draft Day, Revisited: The Small Council's Worst Ideas

The Maye Reach. ICUP took Drake Maye as the first quarterback off the board at pick #24. In the author's other two leagues, Maye went at #58 and #68. Week 1 return: 9.82 points. Eleven picks later, Mover of Chains took Josh Allen as QB2, and Allen scored 35.66. So the first QB taken was not the right one.

The Ladd Lunge. Your moms favorite took Ladd McConkey at #16. In the other leagues he lasted until #37 and #38. He scored 16.7 this week, so fine, we'll let it go for now.

The Namesake Club. nacua matata took Puka Nacua at #6, the third receiver drafted, and named the entire team after him. He scored 9.9. Osama Bin Loveland spent pick #39 on Colston Loveland, the third tight end drafted, and named the entire team after him. He scored 0. Ja'Marr Chase went #3 overall to Moose’s Absent Father and scored 2.2.

The Tight End Trap. Ben timover took Brock Bowers at #31 as the second tight end off the board. Bowers scored 0 and didn't start. Kyle Pitts (#65) also scored 0 for RIP Clippers.

The Special Teams Sweepstakes. Your moms favorite made Brandon Aubrey the first kicker taken, at #112. He scored 1. Ben timover waited until #162 for Evan McPherson, who scored 19. Moose’s Absent Father took the Rams as the first defense at #99 and got 2 points, while your author's Steelers D, a 15th-rounder at #172, scored 20.

The Late-Round Quarterback Graveyard. Here's the big one. Four quarterbacks drafted at #128 or later all put up points on a bench: Patrick Mahomes (#128, 21.66), Jared Goff (#140, 16.44), Jordan Love (#157, 19.48) and Tyler Shough (#163, 23.2). That's 80.78 points of quarterback riding the pine. Maye scored 9.82 in a starting lineup.
