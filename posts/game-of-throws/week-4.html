Everybody Has a Win. Somehow.

Dippin Darts loses by 1.12, the winless club empties, and Bobb Stark keeps the trebuchet in service.

The Clippers have discovered a loophole: apparently you can stay undefeated without reaching 100 points. Dippin Darts more than doubled last week's score and still lost. Both members of the winless club finally escaped, Bobb Stark kept operating the trebuchet, and your author won a game that deserved to end with both managers apologizing.

Week 4 leaves the realm with one undefeated house and no winless teams. This is encouraging news for competitive balance and deeply discouraging news for anyone trying to predict a result.
