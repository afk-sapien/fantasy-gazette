The Bench Was Better

Four goose eggs, a kicker who scored one point, and 203.6 points left rotting on benches. Welcome back, Scripsy Tipsy.

Week 1 is when everyone sets a lineup with total confidence and then watches the bench outscore it. The league left 203.6 points on the pine, four starters put up a flat zero, and three teams would have won with a better lineup. Meanwhile underscore11 dropped 187.3 and made the rest of us look like we drafted blindfolded.

Speaking of drafting blindfolded: this league took a quarterback in the first round, a defense in the third, and a kicker in the sixth. More on that below. First, the carnage.

Draft Day, Revisited

Let's talk about pick 1.09. With eight picks gone and plenty of star running backs and receivers still on the board, aemmar took Caleb Williams, the first quarterback off the board. In the editor's two other leagues, Caleb went 68th and 57th. aemmar took him ninth. And then this happened.

aemmar kept going. The Seahawks defense went at 3.09, the first defense taken, when other leagues waited until picks 122 and 110. Then the first kicker went at 6.02. Here's the problem: in week 1 all three picks worked. Caleb was the league's top scorer, the Seahawks scored 13, McLaughlin scored 11, and aemmar is 1-0. Nobody tell aemmar how drafting is supposed to work.

aemmar had company. sp1cycurry took Matthew Stafford at 3.04 as the second quarterback off the board (other leagues: 118th and 100th), and he scored 5.1. Davante Adams went at 2.07 (other leagues: 54th and 56th) and scored 5.6. BiNG438 took kicker Brandon Aubrey at 6.05, three picks after McLaughlin, and got 1 point.

First-round report: Gibbs (33.6), Bijan (31.3) and Taylor (25.1) were great. Saquon (9), Cook (9.9) and Ja'Marr Chase, the first receiver taken, at 3.2 were not. And the editor's own Brock Bowers, the first tight end taken at 3.10, scored zero. That was a normal price for him in the other leagues, so it's not a reach. It's just a zero.

The late-round steals mostly spent week 1 on the bench. Mahomes (13th round, 22.66), Watson (9th round, 32.7) and Dart (12th round, 26.6) all scored big for teams that didn't start them. The fantasy gods have a sense of humor.
