Maye Set My Lineup, and Maye Should Not Have

Two benched quarterbacks scored 56.96 combined, Josh Allen scored 40 in a loss, and the Van has a head of steam.

Last week this paper said the league drafts well and then sets lineups by coin flip. Week 2 said hold my beer. Matthew Stafford scored 27.98 on a bench. Patrick Mahomes scored 28.98 on a bench, on your author's team, in a loss to a team called Mahome is a Van. And three teams are 2-0, one of them with a perfect lineup.

The good news is that people learned something. Travis Kelce started, Jayden Daniels started and Jalen Coker started. The bad news is that the league still left 110.08 points on benches.
