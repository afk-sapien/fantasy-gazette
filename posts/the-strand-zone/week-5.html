You Couldn't Have Won Anyway

Every loser's best lineup still loses, three managers get every decision right, and the quarterback complaints keep aging badly.

For once, the bench is mostly acquitted. Every losing team in The Strand Zone would still have lost with its best possible lineup. Three managers actually submitted perfect lineups. One of them lost anyway. After a month of pointing at unused points and shouting, this newspaper has been forced to investigate the possibility that sometimes the other team simply has more.

Dirtbag Dynasty remains unbeaten, Maye keeps converting our earlier jokes into evidence against us, and the Sub Station Retirees delivered the week's highest score. Team Osteoporosis has lost four straight, including two under the new name. Team Osteoporosis has successfully demonstrated that a rebrand can preserve every recognizable feature of a failing business.
