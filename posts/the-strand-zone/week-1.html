Arjun Found Jalen Coker, Then Hid Him

Week 1 gave us a 173-point van, a kicker who scored 1, and a waiver steal that never left the bench.

The season is one week old and chaos has already filed its paperwork. Mahome is a Van put up 173.36, the most in the league, without owning a single Patrick Mahomes. Two tight ends scored exactly zero. The first kicker drafted scored 1. And one manager grabbed the best waiver add of the week, watched him score 29.8, and never let him off the bench.

The league averaged 122.64 points and left 164.54 on benches in total. That's a whole extra team's worth of points, and that team would have beaten Unc Dak and the Juniors.

Draft Day, Revisited

Week 1 is the first chance to check the draft against real points. The first round went fine at the top: Gibbs scored 31.1 and Bijan 27.3. Then it got weird. Chase went third and scored 2.2, Puka went fourth and scored 9.9, and Saquon closed the round with 8.5.

The Josh Allen reach. Hailee Steinfeld FC took the first quarterback at pick 19. In the author's other two leagues, Allen lasted until picks 26 and 35. He scored 35.66, so the reach paid off on day one.

The tight end rush. Team Taylor took Brock Bowers as the first tight end, at pick 25. He scored 0 and didn't start. With the very next pick, D.Achane hang low took Trey McBride, who scored 20. Sub Station Retirees took Colston Loveland at pick 32. The other leagues waited until 39 and 53 for him. He started and scored 0.

The kicker run nobody asked for. Brandon Aubrey went first among kickers at pick 109 and scored 1. Cameron Dicker went next at 110 and scored 2. Evan McPherson went 141st and scored 19.

Late-round bargains. Patrick Mahomes went in the 13th round and scored 22.66 on my bench. Caleb Williams in the seventh round scored 37.26. Isaiah Likely in the 12th scored 23.8, also on a bench. Apparently the whole league drafts well and then sets lineups by coin flip.
