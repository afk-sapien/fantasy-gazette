Triple Digits Sold Separately

Four teams miss 100 and one of them wins, Dirtbag Dynasty hits 174.1 with a perfect lineup, and arjunpatelmb loses by 5.84 with nobody at kicker.

Four Strand Zone teams failed to reach 100 points this week. One of them won. Hailee Steinfeld FC scored 83.9 and beat the Professional Ball-Knower's 80.36 in a game that could have been played with the lights off. The Juniors posted the week's low, 75.02, and the Sub Station Retirees managed 95.04 against the one team that remembered to bring a full box of points.

That team was Dirtbag Dynasty. The former Van scored 174.1, which is 65.29 more than the league average and 79.06 more than its opponent. Last week we promised the Retirees a very organized roadblock. The roadblock has been towed. Everybody else spent the week arguing about whether triple digits come standard or cost extra.
